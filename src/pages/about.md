Tim is an enthusiastic software crafter with a big heart for his co-workers. With his 20 years of experience, he's gained some knowledge on how to build well-crafted applications and shares this whenever he can.

His love for Kotlin is a natural extension for a person that cried many tears in NullPointerException and has come to adore strong typed languages.

Outside of work Tim hosts MeetUps for the Belgian Software Crafting & Testing community (SocratesBE), plays board- and videogames, goes on walks with his trusty dog companion and tries to keep his DuoLingo streak going in learning Norwegian.

## About this blog

This blog is built with [Astro](https://astro.build) and hosted for free on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com).
It started out in 2014 as a [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com) site with the [Lanyon](https://github.com/poole/lanyon) theme by [@mdo](https://github.com/mdo).
